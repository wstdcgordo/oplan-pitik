# Pitik
***Own your data. Streamline government. Local-first, Cloud-never.***

[Introduction/Story](#introductionstory) | [Docs](docs/documentation.md) | [Technicals](docs/technicals.md) | [Benchmark/Testing](docs/benchmark.md) | [Ways to Support](#ways-to-support)

Pitik is a high-performance, air-gapped record digitization engine designed primarily for Philippine local government units (LGUs). It automates the transition from paper-based bureaucracy to secure digital databases without the costs, risks, and latency of cloud dependency.

## Introduction/Story
Pitik is born from frustration, just like how some people want to develop applications from an experience or out of curiosity, but this frustration is not just isolated to one person or a few, it's shared: long lines in some city/municipal agencies just to approve the clearance as requirement for a business permit, back and forth to municipal halls just to follow up on overlooked requirements, encoding duration, and surface-level improvement in digitization efforts.

### Why a Localized Architecture Over Cloud Dependencies?

There is a critical operational reason for this architecture.

Many local organizations and government units heavily rely on third-party cloud applications and external software packages to manage public records and complaints. However, this deployment strategy introduces significant vulnerabilities, particularly regarding cloud infrastructure shutdowns, network latency, and systemic cyber threats in an increasingly volatile digital landscape.

Relying on external cloud servers and fragmented applications creates a "cobra effect," where the digital solution introduces new risks, such as high subscription overheads, service interruptions during connectivity blackouts, and critical questions regarding who truly owns and controls the sensitive public data. Furthermore, manual encoding patchworks often defeat the purpose of an online portal, leading to operational bottlenecks.

This repository addresses these challenges by prioritizing an autonomous, less cloud-dependent, and localized database infrastructure.
